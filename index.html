DigiCamFarm

An Android app concept that brings weather, crop prices, loan guidance and farm calculators into one place for Cambodian farmers. It is my graduation project.

Role
UI/UX design, Android development
Platform
Android
Tools
Figma, Kotlin
Status
Design approved, build starting

Weather

Current conditions, an hourly forecast and a 7-day outlook. Urgent advisories, such as heavy rain this afternoon, sit at the top so a farmer can postpone work like fertilizing.

Market prices

Daily crop prices with weekly change and category filters for grains, vegetables and fruits. A banner explains price movements in plain language.

Seller info

Each crop shows its price trend, a 7-day high and low, and demand level, plus verified buyers nearby with a call button.

Loan guidance

Three short questions (farm size, loan purpose, amount) match a farmer with lenders that typically approve loans in their range.

Cost and profit calculator

Pick a crop, enter land, expected yield and costs, and see total cost, total revenue and projected net revenue before planting.

Agriculture tips

Short video guides filtered by topic, such as fertilizer timing and reducing post-harvest losses.

Design decisions

  • One navigation for every task. A five-tab bottom bar (Weather, Farm, Home, Loans, Market) keeps each main feature one tap away.
  • Amber is for attention. Warnings and price alerts use amber against the green interface, so urgent information stands out.
  • Consistent cards. Prices, buyers, loans and weather rows share one card pattern, so users learn it once.
  • Large, clear controls. Big buttons and readable text keep the main actions easy to hit.
Where the project is now

The UI design has been approved and I'm starting the Kotlin implementation. All prices and names in the designs are sample data.
